# User Personas

## Primary Persona: Jonathan Mark C. Bonifacio (Draft)

**Age and role:** 19-year-old freshman student, PUP Sta. Mesa
**Context:** Using ODRS for the first time to request his Informative Copy of Grades (ICG) for his first semester.
**Devices:** Smartphone only. He has no access to a laptop or computer.
**Vision:** Struggles to read small text because of old glasses and eyesight problems.

**Goals**
- Request his first semester's ICG
- Navigate the system comfortably
- Use the system on his phone easily
- Understand the requirements
- Track his request

**Frustrations**
- Text that is too small or hard to read
- Confusing navigation
- Poor mobile responsiveness
- Misleading icons
- Too many unnecessary or redundant buttons

**User need**
"I need to request my first semester's Informative Copy of Grades and track my request."
